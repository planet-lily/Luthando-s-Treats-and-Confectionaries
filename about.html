Our story

Click below to learn more about our story and vision

"Growing up, I have always loved baking and baking has always been a love language at home. I have always been interested in learning how to bake. My mom is also a baker but she was not always keen on experimenting with me so I started experimenting by myself and baking. I used to share all my experiments with my friends and they always enjoyed my treats, so I decided to start selling in high school until now. The aim was to always sell student friendly treats." - Founder , Luthando Mchunu.

To expand the business and be able to supply other places besides Pietermaritzburg. The goal is to one day own a bakery that will continue sell affordable treats to students, residents and batch orders to events.

To offer delicious, fresh and affordable bakes treats. The aim is to make people feel warmth and joy when biting into the treats. To put smiles on customers when they collect or we deliver their treats.
